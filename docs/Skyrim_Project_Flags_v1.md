# Flags from the Skyrim project

Version 1, 2026-09-10. Written in the Skyrim project, `D:\Work\Skyrim_Reborn`, and placed here because these are decisions this project owns and the Skyrim project is waiting on. It is not canon and it is not authoritative over anything in `docs/`. Nothing in it has been applied to any file in this repository.

**Why it exists.** Skyrim has finished reading its book corpus: 1 119 winning BOOK records, every one read, in three rounds ending 2026-09-10. The reading is recorded in `D:\Work\Skyrim_Reborn\20_WorkOrders\WO1_Sources_Reading_v3.md`. Four texts came out of it that Skyrim carries but does not decide, because they are Vvardenfell's matter or because this project has already authored a version of them. Until now those four lived only in Skyrim's documents; this file is the first time they are stated on this side.

**The rule they come from.** Skyrim's reading sorts Morrowind-owned texts into its Tier 3: *the Tribunal, the Sixth House, the Heart and the end of the Dwemer, Red Mountain and the Red Year, Dunmer religion and its ancestors are Vvardenfell's canon and the Morrowind project decides them, whichever plugin carries the text.* Above that sits one further check: **where this project has rewritten a book that Skyrim also carries, whatever its subject, Skyrim mirrors the rewrite.** Skyrim never authors a third version of a text this project has already authored.

**What Skyrim is asking for.** A decision or a confirmation on each of the four, in this project's own time. Nothing here blocks Skyrim: all four are recorded, none is on Skyrim's Floor 1 path yet, and Skyrim will not touch any of them before this project answers.

**What Skyrim is not asking for.** No canon change. *Shared World Canon* is at Rev 6 in both projects, byte-identical apart from line endings, and nothing is owed either way. These four are reading flags, not amendments, and they do not travel the amendment route.

## Flag 1. The Firmament — the exact divergence, measured

**Status: ready to apply, needs only a confirmation.**

Skyrim carries `Book2CommonTheFirmament`, Ffoulke's *The Firmament*, 3 780 characters, defined in `Skyrim.esm`. This project has an authored version at `tools/handwritten/bk_firmament.txt`. The two were compared sentence by sentence on 2026-09-10, and the result is narrower than either project assumed.

**There is exactly one authored difference.** After the Mage entry, the authored version inserts a paragraph the shipped text does not have:

> A note, since no one else troubles to make it. Rain's Hand is not when magicka began. It is when men began to use it, which is a different claim and a much later date. Someone once measured the distance between the two. His figures survive in the Guild's older tables. What he concluded from them does not.

**Everything else that differs is incidental and must not be mirrored.** The sentence-level comparison found three other classes of difference, all of them mechanical:

| Difference | Morrowind | Skyrim | Mirror it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The month of the Lady's Season | Heartfire | Hearthfire | **No.** Each game spells the month its own way; the shipped spelling stands in each |
| Capitalisation of "season" | lower case throughout | "Season" capitalised throughout | **No.** House style, not content |
| The opening drop-cap | "The Stars of Tamriel" | "he Stars of Tamriel" | **No.** Skyrim's book renderer eats the first letter; the record is correct as shipped |

So the mirroring task on Skyrim's side is one insertion of one paragraph, with Skyrim's own spelling and capitalisation left alone.

**What this project decides:** whether that paragraph is final. If it is, Skyrim inserts it and the flag closes. If it is still open here, say so and Skyrim leaves the shipped text untouched until it is.

## Flag 2. Nchunak's Fire and Faith

**Status: needs a decision.**

`DLC2Book4RareNchunaksFireandFaith`, *Nchunak's Fire and Faith*, 1 157 characters, defined in `Dragonborn.esm`. Skyrim's reading places it in Tier 1 by profile — a source that knows — and in Tier 3 by ownership, because its whole subject is the Heart.

A translated account of Nchunak's travels among the Dwemer colonies, explaining Kagrenac's theories. What it carries:

> ...there was but one scholar near who could guide the people through the maze that leads to true misunderstanding.

> ...where neither planar division nor the numeration of amnesia nor any other thing of utility was more valued than the understanding of the self and its relationship to the Heart.

"Planar division", "the numeration of amnesia" and "the understanding of the self and its relationship to the Heart" are Kagrenac's programme stated in the Dwemer's own words. Under the shared canon that is engineering described by people who built it, and it is about the Heart, which is this project's reactor.

**What this project decides:** the wording of this text, if it is to be rewritten at all. Skyrim mirrors whatever is decided and will not touch it otherwise.

## Flag 3. Hanging Gardens

**Status: needs a decision, and it is the more interesting of the pair.**

`Book4RareHangingGardens`, *Hanging Gardens of Wasten Coridale*, 1 114 characters, defined in `Skyrim.esm`. A Dwemer text translated into Aldmeris, surviving in fragments, with a second Dwemer hand correcting the translator at the end. What it carries:

> ...why they did not use solid sound to teach escape from the Earth Bones nor nourished them with frozen flames...

> ...the word I shall have once written of, this "art" our lesser cousins speak of when their admirable ignorance...

> "Put down your ardent cutting-globes, Nbthld. Your Aldmeris has the correct words, but they cannot be properly misinterpreted."

This one already does the work the conversion wants: a Dwemer writer treating magic as *the lesser cousins' art*, named in scare quotes and filed under their ignorance. It is the same position the setting takes, arrived at in the original text without help. Skyrim's judgement was that it probably needs nothing, but the judgement is not Skyrim's to make.

**What this project decides:** whether it stands as written. Skyrim mirrors either way.

## Flag 4. The Warrior's Charge — new, and it changes what Flag 1 means

**Status: new information, raised 2026-09-10, not previously known to either project.**

`SkillConjuration5`, *The Warrior's Charge*, 899 characters, defined in `Skyrim.esm`, an old Redguard poem. It turned up in Skyrim's third reading round, the sweep of texts no keyword screen had matched, which is why it was not known when Flag 1 was first raised.

It is a narrative version of the Firmament's Warrior entry, and the correspondence is exact rather than loose. *The Firmament* states that the Warrior's Charges are the Lady, the Steed and the Lord, and that the Guardians protect their Charges from the thirteenth constellation, the Serpent. The poem walks through precisely that:

> And the Lord of runes, so bored so soon, / Leaves the ship for an evening's dare, / Perchance to wake, the coiled snake...

> And the Lady East, who e'ery beast, / Asleep or a'prowl can rouse a scare, / Screams as her eye, alight in the sky...

> And the mailed Steed, ajoins the deed...

> But the Warrior's blade the Snake unmade, / And the charges wander no more, they swear

The Lord wakes the Serpent, the Lady and the Steed are drawn in, the Warrior kills it and the Charges are safe. Same three Charges, same Guardian, same Serpent, and the poem's title is the Firmament's own term for them.

**Why it matters.** This is the only text in Skyrim's whole book corpus besides *The Firmament* that treats the constellations as anything at all. Flag 1 was raised as a single-book mirroring job; it is not. Whatever this project settles about what the constellations are has a second Skyrim text downstream of it, and that text is a dramatisation of the same relationship, not a passing mention.

The poem states no mechanism and asserts nothing that contradicts anything, so nothing is broken today and Skyrim is changing nothing.

**What this project decides:** nothing directly — the poem is Skyrim's, and Skyrim will judge it. What this project owes is a warning: if a decision here makes the constellations something other than stars, say so, because the answer has to be checked against this poem before Skyrim's Floor 1 touches either text. Skyrim has recorded the dependency at its end.

## For information only: the racial capacity check

No decision needed here, and no action asked. This project's `CLAUDE.md` carries an open check from *Shared World Canon* Rev 6 Part 3 — whether the racial magicka and resistance numbers now have a stated cause that any Morrowind text contradicts. Skyrim carries the same check as index defect 7.

Skyrim's third reading round turned up the two texts on its side that come nearest to the question. Neither is a source that knows and neither argues for anything; both are recorded because they are the plain mortal reading of what Rev 6 Part 3 now calls the density of the Zenad trace.

- `dunPOIWitchNote`, Anise's letter, recruiting a girl to her coven: *"You can feel the power coursing in your blood! You have only to reach out and grasp it!"*
- `FavorIdgrodNote`, Idgrod the Younger to Danica about her brother Joric: the potions ease his nightmares *"but I am afraid my family's gifts run strong in his veins."*

Capacity said to run in blood, twice, by people with no theory of it. If this project's side of the check turns up its Morrowind equivalents, the two lists are worth comparing; that is the whole of the suggestion.
