Zenar. Yes. That is the word, and it is theirs, not ours. I heard it spoken before your grandfathers' grandfathers were born, and I did not understand then what I was being told. They do not name themselves after us, you see. The Temple calls them not-our-ancestors. They call themselves a people, and they call the ones your priests kneel to by a name one letter apart. Think about what that means, and then think about why nobody has told you.
