We're spies. We're the Emperor's hidden eyes and ears in the provinces. We watch the Emperor's enemies. We look for opportunities. We make reports. And, when the Emperor commands, we obey. Here's something you learn early in this trade: what a province calls a thing tells you who is doing the calling. The Temple says Daedra, which means 'not our ancestors.' The things themselves say Zenar. Both words are in my files. Only one of them is a fact. But we're only one of many guilds and factions in Morrowind. You should know a little about Morrowind politics before you get involved with any of these other groups.
