Aedra and Daedra<BR><BR>
<DIV ALIGN="LEFT"><BR><BR>
The designations of Gods, Demons, Aedra, and Daedra are universally confusing to the layman, and are used interchangeably by men who ought to know better.<BR>
<BR>
They are not relative terms. They are Elvish and exact. "Aedra" is usually translated as "ancestor," which is as close as Cyrodilic can come to this Elven concept. "Daedra" means, roughly, "not our ancestors."<BR>
<BR>
Read the two again, and mark what they measure. Not the thing. Us. Where the thing stands to our fire, our table, our dead. They are words of kinship, and kinship is a claim the speaker makes. It is never a property of the one spoken of.<BR>
<BR>
On that claim the Dunmer built a theology. Aedra are stasis; Daedra are change. The Aedra are held to have made the mortal world and to be bound to it, and so may be killed -- witness Lorkhan and the moons. The protean Daedra, for whom the rules do not apply, can only be banished.<BR>
<BR>
Elegant. And in all the centuries of it, no one thought to ask them.<BR>
<BR>
I have asked. Or rather I have listened to those who asked and were not believed: a hermit, a wise woman of the Ashlands, a smuggler who could not be argued out of what he had seen. They do not know one another. They agree. Those we call Daedra call themselves Zenar. Those we call Aedra they call Zenad. One letter, and they insist upon the letter. One people, they say, parted by an old quarrel. Not two kinds of thing.<BR>
<BR>
Which leaves a scholar an awkward sentence to write. Our two great orders of the divine are one word in their mouths, and neither word is ours.<BR>
<BR>
I put this to three priests. Not one disputed the account. Each explained, kindly, why the Temple will go on saying Daedra, and I set their reasoning down without improvement, for it is better than mine. Call a thing kin and you owe it something. Call it not-kin and you may do with it as you please.<BR>
<BR>
The word is not an error. It is a decision, and it is repeated every morning in every chapel on this island.<BR>
