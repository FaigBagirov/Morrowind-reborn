How do you judge a job's difficulty? Consider three things: location, opposition, and payoff. Long walks into the wilderness are usually tougher than local or town tasks. Tough opponents make tough jobs. Zenar, vampires, rogue Telvanni -- you'll learn which ones to look out for. And if Zenar is a new word to you, good. It means you've been reading something besides Temple pamphlets. Ask a wise woman what it means. Don't ask a priest. And finally, if the reward offered is large, the dangers will be large, too.
