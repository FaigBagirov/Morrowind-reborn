How do you know when you've been given a task too tough for you? You have to decide. If it seems too tough, it probably is. Guild stewards and faction leaders don't really know how much you can handle. And some don't care. You have to look out for yourself. But as long as you haven't been given a deadline for completion, it's usually smart to wait and tackle those tough jobs after you're stronger and better equipped. Same goes for words. If you pick up one you don't know -- Zenar, say -- don't take it to a man in a fine robe. Take it to a wise woman out where there aren't any robes.
