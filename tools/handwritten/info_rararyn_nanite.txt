He is the Lord, and Father of the Mountain. He sleeps, but when he wakes, we shall rise from our dreams, shall sweep our land clean of the n'wah. Why have you denied him? As Lord Dagoth has said, "All shall greet him as flesh, or as dust." The ash is not ash. Nanites. Nanites in the ash, in the water, in the walls of this house. They were here before him. They will not stop when he wakes. The Sixth House is risen, and Dagoth is its glory.
